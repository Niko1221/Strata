# The fork's upstream switches on the v0.2.8 engine: prompt-path quality

`tools/quant_quality.py prompt` (20 prompts: 5 segments x 256/512/768/1000 tokens, the batched prompt path, fp16 K/V,
`--gpu-experts`, 1,500 GPU experts), engine 0.1.54 from this repo's `build-hip-oct3`, production config. Reference is
the same engine with the switches off.

| arm | prompts | top-1 | KL mean | median | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| half A: `QFUSE PA_FAST HC_UPMIX HEAD_MIX_MULTI VERIFY_QDEDUP` | 20 | 100.00% | 0.01048 | 0.00064 | 0.06970 |
| half B: `KV_HOST_DMA PF_FUSED PF_GEMM PF_PAD PF_HCDOWN` | 20 | 95.00% | 0.01208 | 0.00108 | 0.09628 |
| all ten | 20 | 100.00% | 0.01382 | 0.00026 | 0.13414 |

The same set on the stdin `run` path (one token per call, CPU experts, fp16 K/V, 4,995 positions) is **bitwise
identical**: top-1 100.00%, KL 0.00000 - the switches do not engage on that path.

One run of the all-ten arm answered nothing on the first 256-token prompt and had to be killed; two halves and a
retry of the full set completed. Not reproduced, no error line in the engine's stderr; treat it as a cold-start flake
until it recurs.

For scale, the KV formats on the same yardstick (`perf/kl-tables.txt`): oct3 with its window 0.0185, k8v4 0.0396
against fp16.
