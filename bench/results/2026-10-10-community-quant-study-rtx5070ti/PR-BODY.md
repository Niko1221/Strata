### What changed in this revision

- **Comprehensive decision methodology:** replaced the limited four-axis index with a complete all-test capability and deployment matrix, an explicit default recommendation and workload-specific alternatives. No arbitrary master score; no new measurement or production-model swap.

- Gyro-S rc2 and Gyro-M rc2 are integrated into the common coding, tools, knowledge/debugging, economics, speed and final-board views — **no standalone model chapter**. Gyro-S current valid rc2 sampled suites, including a scored algebra run, are imported with hashes. Old rc1 data stays archived.
- GPT-6-luna MAX and GPT-6.1-SOL ultra are each integrated as **separate OpenAI-hosted reference rows/bars, score-ordered where comparable, with no medal decoration**; both stay outside local file/arm counts, recommendations, ranks, composites and local performance claims. SOL uses the first-upload result only; the later feedback-assisted Five Bugs validation is excluded.
- Whole tables stay on one page; section headings/explainers stay with their first result. Every table has an automated PDF-page proof.
- Final scorecard and Pick Your File share one evidence-based registry: **Swift IQ3_S (UkisAI), low, is the recommended daily-driver default**. All test families, a new **Speed (Total Wall)** column (code+debug+5-run tool, lower is faster) and deployment caveats are considered; there is no master score, weighting or numeric overall rank. Swift Q2_0 low is the fastest total wall (55m54s) and best stock tool floor; specialist alternatives and experimental forks remain explicitly qualified.
- Former partial coding denominators (155/163, 157/163) are resolved as documented: Base low 155/164 through offline scoring adjudication; Swift IQ3_S low 157/164 through a complete matched run. All 20 local effort rows are included in the final decision matrix, without numeric overall ranks. Gyro-M decode remains unverified only in speed diagnostics; historical Gyro-S rc1 sampled suites are not mixed into rc2 evidence. Historical rc1 coding 153/162 after two HTTP 502s is superseded by the complete rc2 coding runs.

### What we did NOT test

Vision quality; multi-GPU; other GPUs/VRAM sizes; multi-day agents; additional seeds; full knowledge datasets; Gyro-M beyond 131K. Valid rc2 GSM8K, algebra, IFEval, Five Bugs and Needle results are included; additional independent repetitions were not performed. Results are same-rig historical observations, not same-day model-only stock/fork controls. For Luna, MAX is explicitly pinned; unsupported/unverified Chat Completions sampling fields (temperature, presence_penalty, seed, top-p/top-k) are not forwarded by the Codex Responses bridge. For SOL, GSM8K used seed 42, but the original study sampling seed was not retained, so exact sample identity is unverified. MMLU is only the first 100 abstract_algebra questions (5-shot); IFEval is only the first 50 prompts. Tool trials used concurrency 4 versus the reference study concurrency 1. Requested temperature, seed and token budgets are not enforced by Codex CLI. The SOL Codex CLI used a JSON-schema bridge; requested temperature/seed/token budgets were not enforced. Neither hosted result supplies comparable GPU performance; all hosted speed rates are withheld.
