IN A HURRY? Choose a daily driver, not a benchmark trophy

  • Recommended default: S-IQ3_S (UkisAI) low on the stock study setup. This is a reasoned choice across the full evidence matrix, not a master-score winner or a command to change the deployed model.
  • Broad capability: Swift IQ3_S low scored 157/164 coding, first 92.7%, in 13m52s; tool mean 88.2 and all-five floor 76.1. Its per-file samples scored math 98%, algebra 85%, instructions 84.0/89.5% prompt/instruction, hidden debugging 20/25, and retrieval 100% at 260,096 effective tokens.
  • Choose the trade-off: Swift Q2_0 low is the fastest total workload (55m54s code+debug+tool) and has the best stock tool floor, with lower sampled knowledge/debug scores; Swift IQ3_XXS low completes coding fastest (62m31s total); Q-IQ3_S xhigh has the highest observed stock final coding score but takes far longer (~160m). The recommended default is mid-pack on speed (75m03s), chosen for breadth.
  • Fork results stay visible, not silently promoted: Gyro-S rc2 has valid coding/tools, math 96%, algebra 79%, IFEval 76.0/82.9%, debugging 21/25 visible and 16/25 hidden, and 100% retrieval at 129,024 tokens. Gyro-M has narrow memory headroom and campaign/safety caveats. Neither is the default recommendation.
  • All testing contributes: coding/repair, five-run tools and hard chains, instruction following, sampled math/algebra, visible/hidden debugging, context retrieval, actual task times and deployment evidence. TPS is separate diagnostics, not a daily-driver score.
  • Confidence is limited: small sampled suites and one rig do not establish universal model superiority, multi-day stability, comprehensive safety or a model-only causal difference. No fabricated missing values and no arbitrary master score.

THE FOUR QUESTIONS — practical trade-offs

Which default?

S-IQ3_S (UkisAI) low: broad stock-cohort capability with a short complete coding job.
Benchmark-backed recommendation, not long-term stability certification.

Which specialist?

S-Q2_0 (UkisAI) low for the tool floor and fastest total wall (56m); S-IQ3_XXS (UkisAI) low for coding latency.
A specialist advantage is not a universal default win.

Why not crown the fork?

Gyro-S offers a strong tool floor but lower observed algebra/instruction/debug scores and shorter tested recall capacity than the stock default. Gyro-M has narrow 16 GB margin, failed raw gates and campaign caveats.
Capability and deployment suitability are separate decisions.

How was the choice made?

The final matrix displays all test families, total wall time and limitations; the default rationale is explicit.
No weights, numeric overall rank or invented master score. Speed is shown as total wall, not folded into a score.
