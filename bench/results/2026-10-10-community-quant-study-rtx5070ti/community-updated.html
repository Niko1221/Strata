IN A HURRY? The study in six lines

  • Best measured stock all-rounder: Q-Q2_0 (ISTA-DASLab) xhigh is #1 on the corrected eligible scoreboard (72). This is a seven-axis choice aid, not a universal intelligence score.
  • Pick the specialist when one axis matters. Stock agent reliability: S-Q2_0 (UkisAI) low (78.3 floor). Fast coding: S-IQ3_XXS (UkisAI) low (11m56s). Those lower-composite picks do not contradict the all-rounder.
  • Coding ceilings are close; costs are not. Highest complete reported score 96.3%: Q-IQ3_S (ISTA-DASLab) xhigh, Gyro-S (AgentionAI) low. Swift IQ3_S low reported the same percentage with only 163 scored tasks and is qualified, not tied on equal coverage.
  • Tool support is a serving-stack fact. Gyro-M / rc2 joined the same pinned tool evaluator at 86.0 low and 87.8 xhigh. Gyro-S / rc1 remains n/a for tools. That is not an isolated model-only comparison.
  • More thinking has mixed returns. Gyro-M xhigh solved one additional coding task after repair at 4.8x wall and 4.9x tokens. Its Hard Mode mean rose, but its floor fell from 78.3 to 71.7.
  • Fit and validity are part of the recommendation. Gyro-M is experimental on this 16 GB card: 205 MiB minimum free VRAM, 78 C peak, invalid-slice status. No daily-driver endorsement. Partial scoring and test failures remain disclosed.
