## Validation, failures and limits

Passed: both unmodified official suites, 36 stored outcomes each, zero failures/skips. Separate independent per-suite audits match every stored record uniquely and in schedule order to the full native log, including counts, reuse, timers, draft counters and source-defined rate precision. Saved medians/ranges were rebuilt independently. All three main GGUF files match exact immutable upstream LFS identities: [MODEL_IDENTITIES.json](MODEL_IDENTITIES.json). The Flash narrow 12-case correctness score belongs to the separate draft pilot; these quick suites do not test correctness.

Failed: an earlier Qwen3.6 setup attempt at 131072 context exhausted GPU memory during startup, before any benchmark request. Its [engine log](qwen36-128k-startup-failure/engine.log) and [configuration](qwen36-128k-startup-failure/config.json) are retained. This failed capacity attempt is separate from the new successful 65536-context suite. No request was retried inside either new suite.

Not measured: general answer quality, broad full-window comprehension, inference process RAM peak, continuous VRAM peak, energy per token, equal-quality ranking or an isolated version/kernel improvement. Client concurrency was not requested. [Sampled resources](sampled-official-resources.json) records the process windows, sample counts and sampled extrema; samples include loading/warmup/shutdown and are not continuous peaks. Reported TTFT/total latency uses the client clock, while prompt/decode rates use the separate engine timers. Reasoning is disabled for the quick workload. All warm-up/cache conditions and individual outcomes remain visible.

Use the exact released Terminal/engine and model hashes. Each suite's `command.json` is the complete invocation array, `config.json` the source configuration, and `predeclared-provenance.json` its before-start identities. Replace `<terminal>`, `<engine-component>`, `<campaign>` and `<model-store>` with actual local paths. Run the official array against a new dedicated loopback engine; preserve the fixed three repetitions and `--long`. All complete native logs and per-request JSON are attached. Flash uses the same attached custom template as the separate draft report; Qwen3.6 uses its pack tokenizer from released setup. No model weights or generated packs are submitted.

The prior withdrawn reports remain in owner evidence and branch history. They are replaced by these complete fresh runs rather than combined with them or selectively reused. Private historical/context comparisons and Avalon experiments are excluded from this results-only submission.
