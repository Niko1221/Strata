Pool

Several PCs as one: share the requests (each PC runs the whole model), or split one model's layers across them (each runs its own layers, with their experts in its own RAM and VRAM).

–
This PC's role

Pooling does not add up the speed of the PCs. Each new word passes through the model's layers one after another, so one chat always runs at about one PC's speed.
  • Share requests: every PC runs the whole model on its own. Two chats at once run on two PCs at full speed each; a chat stays on the PC that holds its conversation.
  • Split layers: the PCs divide the model's layers, and each keeps its layers' experts in its own RAM and VRAM and its share of the context. The pool caches more experts, and fits a model or context one PC could not. The PCs take turns on every word, so a chat gains only from fewer cache misses, and a slow GPU or network slows it down.
