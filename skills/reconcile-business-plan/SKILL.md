# Reconcile Business Plan

Identify the audience, scenario, date, currency and units. Read current record revisions and shared variables. Tag amounts as actuals, estimates, assumptions, or unknown. Recalculate linked totals and cash timing from evidence. Check cross-document consistency after any variable changes and show scenario drivers and sensitivity.

Present material gaps with the decision each prevents. Keep approved inputs, forecasts and submission-ready documents identifiable. Route an authorized change to the owned variable or record and verify the resulting documents through fresh readback.
