# Prepare Business Decision

Name the decision, accountable owner, evidence date, affected plans and audience. Link each material claim to its current source revision and give the calculation or evidence behind it. Document missing approvals, attachments, and unresolved facts alongside their impact. Label a working draft with the exact remaining decisions needed for submission.

Apply the project's approval path before changes to strategy or external submission. Preserve source versions and disclose the status of each required document.
