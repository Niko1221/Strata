# Design Science Study

State the question, outcome, alternative explanations, and decision the result will inform. Record protocols, source versions, inclusion criteria, and measurement units. Set comparison and failure criteria before looking at the results when feasible. Distinguish hypotheses, direct observations, derived values and interpretation in the report.

Give negative and missing results explicit status. Preserve raw evidence and reproducible transforms. Map findings to the studied setting and identify the next observation that would materially change the conclusion.
