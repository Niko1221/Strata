# Curate Science Evidence

For each selected claim, identify primary source, original bytes or stable reference, revision and supporting location. Track provenance, translation, interpretation, and publication status. If a source or canonical route is unavailable, retain the gap and its effect on the claim. Check rendered pages before delivering a reading edition.

Publish revised canonical content only through the authorized owner. Label historical copies and translated reading editions with their source date and revision. Deliver a compact manifest linking claims and documents to actual evidence.
