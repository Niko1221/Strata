# Verify a data migration

Pin source identity, snapshot revision, route bindings, expected schema, and artifact digests. Use an isolated destination and confirm which targets are permitted to change. For a restore designed for an empty target, verify that precondition and reject a populated destination. Record source and destination identities in the receipt.

Check schema and migrations, representative bodies and relationships, stable IDs, and content fingerprints in addition to counts. Verify the consumer's project-routed readback. Keep a rollback or recovery point with the evidence. Apply live activation only when authorized and after the relevant isolated checks.
